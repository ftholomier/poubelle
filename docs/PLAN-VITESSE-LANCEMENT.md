# Vitesse au lancement : mesures et plan d'optimisation (octobre 2026)

Ouverture prévue vers le 25/12/2026, boutique comprise. Mesures faites sur un banc proche de la
production (Apache 2.4 + PHP-FPM 8.3, 8 processus PHP, OPcache, 4 processeurs) avec le
`.htaccess` du site, puis audit du code en six axes relu par des contre-vérificateurs.

## Mesures de référence (avant optimisation)
- Temps serveur, cache chaud : 5 à 35 ms par page ; sur les 3 637 adresses du plan du site,
  médiane 14 ms, 90 % sous 20 ms, 99 % sous 45 ms. À froid (OPcache vide) : accueil 255 ms.
- Capacité : 215 à 246 pages HTML par seconde, sans erreur jusqu'à 200 connexions simultanées ;
  au-delà, l'attente s'allonge (mélange de 25 pages : 37 ms à 10 connexions, 382 ms à 100,
  765 ms à 200).
- Lighthouse mobile : accueil 77 (LCP 5,4 s : les 5 photos du diaporama chargées d'un coup,
  affiche du teaser 185 Ko, polices 227 Ko) ; autres pages 85 à 97.

## Déjà corrigé (octobre 2026)
- **Teaser servi par Apache** (`teaser_src()`) : copie à adresse versionnée dans
  `public/assets/video/`, plages d'octets et cache d'un an ; avant, huit visiteurs lents
  bloquaient tous les processus PHP (aucune page servie pendant 20 s sur le banc).
- **Limiteur de débit en 256 fichiers par compteur** (`RateLimiter`) : une requête ne verrouille
  et ne réécrit que le sien, rien n'est écrit pour une demande refusée ; 0,4 ms par appel avec
  20 000 visiteurs (avant : coût proportionnel au nombre de visiteurs, ex. choix des cookies
  389 → 44 par seconde à 10 000 adresses).
- **Choix des cookies** : plus de limitation par adresse (un seul envoi par nouveau visiteur),
  journal du mois plafonné à 200 Mo.
- **Compression du JavaScript** (`text/javascript` ajouté au `.htaccess`) : site.js 22,9 → 7,6 Ko.
- Le service worker ne garde jamais la vidéo.
- **Calculs en cache faits une seule fois** (`Memo`, `Chiffres`) : quand un cache est périmé
  (toutes les 5 minutes, après un enregistrement), un seul processus recalcule ; les autres
  visiteurs reçoivent aussitôt la version précédente (8 visiteurs simultanés : 1 calcul au lieu
  de 8, 7 réponses immédiates). Sans version précédente, ils attendent puis relisent.
- **Vignettes protégées** (`Images::make`) : verrou par image (10 demandes simultanées, une seule
  fabrication), au plus 2 fabrications à la fois sur tout le site ; au-delà, redirection non
  gardée vers la taille déjà prête la plus proche. Préparation complète avant l'ouverture :
  `php bin/console.php images all` (fichiers déjà prêts sautés, relançable).

## Reste à faire avant le lancement
Les points P0, P1 et OPS du plan ci-dessous, sauf ceux corrigés plus haut : la préparation
complète des vignettes sur le serveur avant l'ouverture (OPS-2), le diaporama de l'accueil
(P1-4), la recherche bornée (P1-6), puis le cache des pages pour les visiteurs anonymes (P1-1,
le plus gros gain de capacité) et, la boutique ouvrant au lancement, les points boutique de la
section 5 qui deviennent P1.

# Sochaux Rétro: ranked optimization plan for the launch

## 0. Summary

- **Render speed is not the problem; worker starvation is.** Warm pages take 5–35 ms and the sitemap median is 14 ms. The bench saturates at about 215–246 HTML pages/s on 4 CPUs (about 13 ms of PHP CPU plus about 3 ms of Apache/gzip per page), with zero errors up to 200 connections.
- **Four mechanisms can take the site down at launch.** Each one either blocks a PHP worker without using CPU, or serializes all workers on one lock:
  1. The 28 MB teaser is streamed by PHP. Eight slow viewers caused a total outage on the bench: 0 home pages served in 20 s.
  2. Several JSON files are rewritten under a single global lock on every request: rate limiter, consent, Rétro-Direct presence, push opens, 404 log. Their cost grows with the audience: search fell from 118 to 80 req/s, and consent from 389 to 44 req/s at 10k IPs.
  3. Derived caches have no lock, so every concurrent request recomputes them at once (Memo, Chiffres, Media, missing caches). Pages took 0.3–4 s during these recomputes, and they recur every 5 minutes and after every back-office save.
  4. About 50% of image variants are missing and are generated by GD on first view, with no lock.
- **Order of work:**
  - The **P0** items remove these failure modes. They are small to medium changes and must ship before launch.
  - The **full-page cache for anonymous HTML** (section 3) is the only change that multiplies HTML capacity: about 14× less server time per hit, and an estimated 4× more pages/s on the bench, where Apache compression becomes the limit. I recommend it as **P1**, behind a kill switch, built after the P0 items. Cache misses still go through the P0 code paths.
  - The **OPS** items cost no code but are mandatory: OPcache sizing on o2switch, thumbnail pre-generation, warm-up, a launch-window freeze, and a paced opening push.

### How the findings were filtered

The `verified` arrays in the audit input were all `null`, so no code-truth or impact verdicts reached me. I re-read the code for every item kept in P0, P1 and OPS. The path:line references below come from my own reading, and ✔ marks an item whose claim I confirmed in the code.

I found the skeptic run outputs in the scratchpad and used three of them to calibrate impact:
- `skeptic-memo/run.out`: 8 parallel requests at murs-photos expiry took 2.5–3.6 s, with 16 rewrites.
- `skeptic-hero/ab-results.json`: on slow 4G, LCP went from 2,600 to 2,368 ms, page load from 5.6 to 3.2 s, and hero bytes from 543 to 81 KB.
- `skeptic-static/runs*.txt`: the gains from PHP-compiling the JSON files were lost in run-to-run noise, so that item is demoted to P2.

Corrections to the audit:
- **PhpCache forced invalidation.** data-concurrency proposed dropping the forced invalidation in `PhpCache::read`. That is unsafe in `update()`: the CLI and FPM do not share OPcache invalidations, so a CLI write followed by an FPM update could lose data. Only the `remember()` waiter path may skip it (P0-3).
- **Consent bucket.** The limiter allows 30 hits per IP per hour, not per visitor. I kept the finding and recommend removing the limiter on this route.

## 2. Before-launch items (P0 / P1)

### P0-1. Serve the teaser video and poster statically

**Merges:** request-path #4, http-static #1 and #4, frontend #1, association-shop #5 (video part).

**Problem (✔):**
- Routes:
  - museum: `app/Kernel.php:175` → `app/Front/Pages.php:445-457`;
  - association: `app/Vitrine/Pages.php:274-283`, always on when the file exists (`app/Vitrine/Pages.php:18`).
- Both serve the video through `Response::media`, and `send()` streams it with `readfile` or a 256 KB `fread`/`echo` loop (`app/Core/Response.php:98-119`). The worker stays busy until the client has read every byte.
- The poster `/video/teaser.jpg` (1920×1080, 185 KB) is fetched through PHP on every first home view (`templates/home.php:82-83`).
- Neither file has an ETag or Last-Modified.

**Change:**
- When the teaser becomes public (`home.teaser` on and the site open, `waiting.teaser` on, or the association home), copy `app/Resources/video/teaser.mp4` to `public/assets/video/teaser-<sha1:8>.mp4`.
- Publish a poster at 960 or 1280 px wide as `teaser-<hash>.webp` (44–60 KB, against 185 KB today).
- Make the copies at the moment the setting is saved, and in `Updater::refresh`.
- Point these templates at the hashed URLs:
  - `templates/home.php:82-83`
  - `templates/waiting.php:51-52`
  - `templates/vitrine/home.php:27-28`
  - `templates/vitrine/waiting.php:103`
- `public/.htaccess`:
  - add `AddType video/mp4 .mp4`;
  - add `mp4` to the immutable FilesMatch at line 31.
- `public/sw.js`:
  - add `/assets/video/` to `BYPASS` (line 31);
  - otherwise `cacheFirst` at line 137 would cache a full GET of the video.
- Keep the PHP routes only for the team preview of a closed site, and answer a 302 to the hashed URL when the teaser is public.

**Gain:** removes the only measured total-outage mode. In e-video-8slow (8 viewers), the home page went from 0 rps with 20/20 timeouts to the reference 235 rps. It also removes one PHP hit and about 140 KB per first home view.

**Risk and safeguard:**
- The hashed file is reachable before opening if it is copied too early: create it only when the teaser is shown.
- Old `/video/teaser.*` links must 302 to the new URLs.
- Test: `node run-all.js after --only=load --scenarios=e-video-slowclients`. The 8-slow-client run must keep home throughput within 10% of the reference, and `ls -l /proc/<fpm>/fd | grep teaser` must show nothing.

**Effort:** S.

### P0-2. O(1) rate limiter, and no limiter on fire-and-forget beacons

**Merges:** request-path #5; data-concurrency #2; api-live #1, #2, #8; frontend #4; association-shop #3, #13 (limiter part); the limiter half of the Rétro-Direct and push findings.

**Problem (✔):**
- `RateLimiter::hit` (`app/Core/RateLimiter.php:27-51`) runs `JsonStore::update`. That takes a blocking `flock` (`app/Core/JsonStore.php:111-121`), decodes the whole bucket, filters the timestamps of every key (lines 36-41), pretty-prints the JSON (`JsonStore.php:47`) and renames the file.
- It does this on every hit, refused hits included (line 47 always writes).
- Hot buckets:
  - `search` (`app/Front/Api.php:62`, on every keystroke);
  - `consent` (`Api.php:88`, one per new visitor, 1-hour window);
  - `retro` (`app/Front/Retro.php:206`);
  - `push` and `push-ouverture` (`app/Front/Appli.php:52`, `:89`);
  - `fil-jaune`, `quiz`, `onze` and `chat-day`.

**Change:**
- In `RateLimiter::hit`, `clear` and `remaining` (same API):
  - shard each bucket into 256 files: `storage/ratelimit/{bucket}/{id[0..1]}.json`;
  - lock the shard only;
  - write compact JSON;
  - **write nothing when the hit is refused**;
  - clean expired entries only in the shard that was touched.
- A drop-in prototype exists in `scratchpad/perf2026/api-live/tools/RateLimiter.sharded.php`.
- Keep the IPv6 /64 grouping of `RateLimiter::id()`. Keep the exact sliding window, so login, gate, don and newsletter keep their current behaviour.
- Remove the limiter from `/api/consentement`. The handler only appends one bounded line (`Api.php:104`). Bound abuse instead with a size cap on the monthly `storage/consent/*.jsonl` (stop appending above, say, 200 MB).
- Remove the limiter from `push-ouverture`. Its counter is cosmetic; see P1-7.
- Optional: use APCu (`apcu_inc`) when o2switch provides it.
- Cron `menage`: delete shard files untouched for more than 2 days.

**Gain (prototype, measured on the isolated copy):**

| Endpoint | Load | Before | After |
|---|---|---|---|
| Search suggest | 6,000+ IPs | 77 rps | 701–773 rps |
| Consent | 10k+ IPs | 44 rps | 2,000–2,400 rps |
| Refused (429) responses | — | 77–93 ms | about 1–3 ms |

The baseline d2 scenario (80 rps, p50 1.25 s at 100 connections) should become CPU-bound again.

**Risk and safeguard:**
- Correctness under concurrent writers is kept by the per-shard flock.
- Re-run the tests covering forms, login, push and the newsletter.
- Run `api-live/tools/rl-micro.php`: cost per hit must not depend on the number of keys (target under 0.3 ms at 20k keys).
- Re-run d2-api-search-manyip: rps must not fall as connections rise.

**Effort:** S–M.

### P0-3. Single-flight and stale-while-revalidate for derived caches

**Merges:** request-path #1 and #2; data-concurrency #1, #3 (code part), #4, #5, #6, #7; api-live #9.

**Problem (✔):**
- **Memo** (`app/Core/Memo.php:15`, `:34`, `:37`, `:39`):
  - the TTL is 300 s, and an expired entry is recomputed with no lock by every concurrent request;
  - each request then rewrites the PHP file, including the 2.2 MB `murs-photos` and 0.9 MB `fil-jaune-reseau` files, and every rewrite wastes OPcache memory;
  - almost every stamp includes `index-2.php`, so every back-office save restarts the stampede everywhere.
- **Chiffres** (`app/Services/Chiffres.php`):
  - `cached()` (`:115-124`) → `refreshLater()` (`:127-141`) registers a full 1.1–1.3 s rebuild after the response, with no lock and no re-check, in every request of the stale window;
  - `/chiffres/` → `all()` (`:98-109`) rebuilds synchronously;
  - the signature (`:191-198`) includes both `index-2.php` and `derived.php`, so each save opens two stale windows;
  - `Updater::afterChange` deletes `chiffres-*.json`.
- **PhpCache::remember** (`app/Core/PhpCache.php:61-64` → `update`, `:85-91`): waiters queue behind the lock, then each calls `read()`, which force-invalidates (`:29-31`) and recompiles the 4–11 MB file one after the other.
- **PhotoWall** (`app/Services/PhotoWall.php:133-138`): with no usage index, it calls `Media::rebuildUsage()` → `Derived::rebuild()` (`app/Data/Media.php:142-145`), which locks and rebuilds unconditionally (`app/Data/Derived.php:227-236`). Every wall request in flight therefore rebuilds in turn.
- **Usage file** (`app/Data/Derived.php:945`): `Media::saveUsage()` (`app/Data/Media.php:124-128`) always rewrites `media-usage.json`, which invalidates the `murs-photos` memo after every build.
- **Media buckets** (`app/Data/Media.php:52-74`): a stale bucket makes the request re-read the 7.4 MB `media.json` and rewrite 18 files, with no lock. `Media::put` writes `media.json` before the caches, which opens that window on every media edit.

**Change, one PR with six parts:**

1. **Memo**
   - Remove the TTL. Store the sorted `publish_at` timestamps of fiches with status `planifie` in `index-2.php` (the only time-dependent rule is at `app/Data/Index.php:236`), and add to the stamp the count of those whose time has passed. Memos then change exactly when a scheduled fiche goes live.
   - On a stamp mismatch with an existing file: `flock(LOCK_EX|LOCK_NB)` on `{file}.lock`. The winner recomputes, after `Response::detach()` for murs-photos and fil-jaune. Others return the previous value.
   - Block only when no file exists.
   - Write only if `md5(var_export)` changed.
2. **Chiffres**
   - Use a `LOCK_NB` lock in `refreshLater()` and in `all()`, then re-read and re-check the signature after acquiring it.
   - `/chiffres/` serves the stale file when the lock is busy.
   - Base the signature on `Derived::built()` plus `en.json` only, which gives one wave per save instead of two.
   - Add `chiffres-*.json` to `KEEP_CACHES` (the `V` constant already invalidates them on format changes).
   - Call `Chiffres::warm()` at the end of the after-page `Derived::build` and in `Updater::refresh()`.
3. **PhpCache::remember**
   - Before locking, use a plain `include` if the file exists.
   - After the lock: `clearstatcache(true, $file)`; if the file now exists, release the lock and use a plain `include`, with no forced invalidation.
   - Keep the forced invalidation in `update()`: it prevents lost updates between the CLI and FPM.
4. **PhotoWall**
   - Never call `Derived::rebuild()` from a web request. Return the walls without the missing usage, call `Derived::markDirty()` to start the single-flight after-page rebuild, and serve the result next time.
   - Add the same guard on `Derived::part()` (`app/Data/Derived.php:94-99`).
5. **saveUsage and media caches**
   - `saveUsage()` and the Media group files are written only when their hash changes.
6. **Media::put / putMany**
   - Write the cache files first and `media.json` last.
   - On the stale path, use a `LOCK_NB` lock and serve the existing bucket.

**Gain:**
- Removes the warm-crawl spikes (179–430 ms on category pages).
- Removes the 8×(3.1–4.3 s) stampede at murs-photos expiry, 2.5–3.6 s in the skeptic re-run.
- Removes 300–339 ms ×8 player pages at fil-jaune expiry.
- Removes 36 Chiffres builds for 6 saves, and about 25 concurrent 1.1 s rebuilds per language when the home receives 20 req/s after a save.
- OPcache waste: about 57 MB/h from TTL rewrites today; target flat.

**Risk and safeguard:**
- Values can be stale for one compute duration.
- A scheduled publication must appear on time: test with a fiche set to `planifie` 2 minutes ahead.
- Tests:
  - `scratchpad/perf2026/stampede.sh`: 8 parallel requests at expiry must stay under 30 ms, with exactly 1 rename per memo (strace);
  - after a save, count Chiffres builds per pid (target 1 per language);
  - delete `index-2.php` and `media-usage.json` on a copy, fire 16 concurrent requests, and check that exactly 1 build runs;
  - `tests/perf.php` stays green.

**Effort:** M.

### P0-4. Thumbnails: per-file lock and GD concurrency cap

**Merges:** data-concurrency #12, http-static #2, frontend #3. Pre-generation is OPS-2.

**Problem (✔):**
- `Images::serve` (`app/Services/Images.php:48-55`) generates the variant whenever the file is missing, with no lock. N concurrent misses produce N GD decodes.
- `open()` raises `memory_limit` to 1536M for each decode (`:135`).
- Baseline: 49.6% of referenced variants are missing; one 320w generation took 573 ms.

**Change:**
- Around the existence check and `generate()`, take `flock` on a bucketed lock file (`storage/cache/img-locks/{crc32 % 64}.lock`, outside `public/`), then re-check with `clearstatcache`.
- Add a global semaphore of 2 slots (`LOCK_NB` on `img-slot-{0,1}.lock`). When both are busy, answer 302 with `Cache-Control: no-store` to the nearest existing width, or to the placeholder.
- Apply the same lock in `derivative()`, which is used by the PDF export.

**Gain:** caps image CPU at 2 concurrent GD jobs whatever the traffic, and removes duplicate work, which is what risks the memory cap of the shared account (LVE).

**Risk and safeguard:**
- The fallback redirect must stay `no-store`; otherwise the browser keeps the wrong size for a year (immutable cache).
- Test: delete one 1600w WebP on a copy, burst 20 concurrent requests, and count GD runs (target 1).

**Effort:** S.

### P1-1. Full-page micro-cache (design in section 3)

**Merges:** request-path #3. The prerequisites are P1-2 and P1-3; P0-3 must ship first.

### P1-2. CSP hash on public layouts

**Problem (✔):**
- The CSP sets a fresh nonce per request (`app/Kernel.php:104`, `app/helpers.php:72-76`).
- The only public inline scripts are two constant strings: `templates/layout.php:61` and `templates/vitrine/layout.php:44`. JSON-LD is not executed, so the CSP does not apply to it.
- Nonces are otherwise used only in `templates/admin/*`.

**Change:**
- Define both scripts as PHP constants, used by the templates and by `Kernel::csp()`.
- Outside `/admin`, emit `script-src 'self' 'sha256-…'`. Keep the nonce for `/admin` only.

**Gain:** responses become byte-identical, which allows an ETag and 304 answers, and the page cache no longer has to reuse a nonce.

**Safeguard:** no CSP error in the browser console on the museum layout, the association layout and the admin. Two consecutive anonymous `GET /saisons/` return identical bodies.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-3. Do not touch the session for anonymous visitors

**Merges:** request-path #7, association-shop #9.

**Problem (✔):**
- `ShopPages::visible()` (`app/Shop/ShopPages.php:30-33`) calls `Auth::user()` on every page through the header and footer (`templates/partials/header.php:52,54,168`, `footer.php:39`).
- When the visitor has an `sr_session` cookie (any form page sets one), `Auth::user()` (`app/Core/Auth.php:69`) opens and locks the session, and PHP adds `no-store`.
- A forged or expired cookie creates a new session file on every request, because `use_strict_mode` is on (`app/Core/Session.php:18`).

**Change:**
- Set an `sr_team=1` hint cookie at login (HttpOnly, Secure, SameSite=Lax) and clear it at logout and on invalidation.
- In `Auth::user()`, return null when there is no active session and no `sr_team` cookie.
- Call `session_cache_limiter('')` in `Session::start()`, and send `private, no-store` explicitly on form pages and the cart.

**Gain:**
- No session I/O or lock for these visitors.
- Pages stay cacheable by the browser and the service worker.
- Bots replaying cookies stop creating files.
- More of these visitors can use the page cache.

**Safeguard:**
- With a cookie jar taken from `/contact/`, a player page opens no `sess_*` file in strace and sends no `no-store`.
- Staff log in once to get the hint cookie; the team bar and the closed-shop preview still appear for them.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-4. Hero slider: load only the slide that is shown

**Problem (✔):** all 5 slides are stacked inside the viewport (`public/assets/css/site.css:560`: absolute, opacity 0), so `loading="lazy"` on slides 1-4 (`templates/home.php:14`) has no effect. `home.js` never sets `src`.

**Change:**
- Slides 1-4 get `data-src` and `data-srcset` in place of `src` and `srcset`.
- `home.js` fills in the next slide after the `load` event plus `requestIdleCallback`, and again in `go()`. The arrows fill in their target slide before switching.

**Gain (skeptic A/B on slow 4G):**
- hero bytes 543→81 KB;
- page load 5.6→3.2 s;
- LCP −9% (2,600→2,368 ms).

It also triggers 4 fewer cold-derivative generations per home view: 295 of the 613 slider-pool images have no 800w file locally.

**Safeguard:** run Lighthouse mobile on `/` (3 runs) and check the slider crossfade by hand: the next slide must be loaded about 5 s before it is shown.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-5. Static icons, cheap 404, append-only 404 log

**Merges:** request-path #6, data-concurrency #13, http-static #7.

**Problem (✔):**
- Every GET 404 runs `Redirects::find`, which decodes the 494 KB `redirects.json`.
- `Redirects::log404` (`app/Kernel.php:230-236`, `app/Data/Redirects.php:60-81`) rewrites `storage/404.json` under an exclusive lock, before the page is sent.
- The exclusion regex does not cover `.ico`, `.png` or `/assets/`.
- `public/` has no `favicon.ico` and no `apple-touch-icon*.png`.

**Change:**
- Ship `public/favicon.ico`, `public/apple-touch-icon.png` and `public/apple-touch-icon-precomposed.png`.
- In `public/.htaccess`, before the fallback to `index.php`:
  ```
  RewriteCond %{REQUEST_FILENAME} !-f
  RewriteRule ^(assets/|favicon\.ico$|apple-touch-icon) - [R=404,L]
  ```
- Make `log404` append one line (`FILE_APPEND|LOCK_EX`, after `Response::detach()`) to `storage/404/YYYY-MM-DD.log`, and aggregate it into `404.json` from the cron. The back-office screen keeps the same file format.

**Gain:** icon probes go from 10–15 ms of PHP plus a locked 600 KB rewrite to served by Apache. The cap of about 160 404/s for the whole site disappears.

**Safeguard:** `curl /favicon.ico` returns 200 from Apache. The mtime of `storage/404.json` no longer changes on a miss. Routes that legitimately have extensions keep working: `/robots.txt`, `/sitemap.xml`, `/partage/*.png`, `/pdf/*`, `agenda.ics`.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-6. Bound the cost of search and suggestions

**Merges:** data-concurrency #10; api-live #5, #6, #15 (empty query); frontend #5.

**Problem (✔):**
- `Search::query` caps the query at 120 characters but not the number of tokens (`app/Services/Search.php:228-265`), and runs a second full OR pass when the AND pass finds nothing.
- `/recherche/` has no rate limit.
- The client sends a request after 120 ms of debounce, and immediately on an empty query, with no minimum length (`public/assets/js/site.js:127-133`). Responses are `no-store`.

**Change:**
- **Server:**
  - at most 6 tokens;
  - ignore 1-character tokens unless alone;
  - scan bodies only for tokens of 3 characters or more;
  - no OR fallback when there are more than 3 tokens;
  - cap `limit` at 20;
  - suggestions under 2 characters return the static defaults;
  - suggestions are sent with `Cache-Control: public, max-age=300`, since they hold no personal data;
  - `/recherche/` goes through the O(1) limiter (30 per minute per IP).
- **Client:**
  - 250 ms debounce;
  - no request under 2 characters;
  - an in-memory Map from query to results;
  - the empty-query suggestions cached in `sessionStorage`.

**Gain:**
- Worst case goes from 1.3–1.46 s per request to about 30 ms or less.
- Typing a word costs 3 or fewer requests instead of 6–10.
- Removes a one-client DoS: 8 crafted requests saturate the bench.

**Safeguard:** check recall on the 20 most common queries before and after (diff of the result lists). The assistant's retrieval (`Rag.php`) also calls `Search::query`, so test the canned questions.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-7. Opening-push click herd, and the in-request test push

**Merges:** api-live #8, association-shop #13, data-concurrency #9 (open-counting part).

**Problem (✔):**

- Pacing of the opening send is an operational step (OPS-5).

**Gain:** clicks go from 311→88 rps (6k clicks) to a flat O(1) cost, and a public click can no longer hold a worker for 15 s.

**Safeguard:** the open counter shown in the back-office becomes eventually consistent. Update `tests/notifications.php`.

**Effort:** S.

### P1-8. Updater: no global OPcache reset, keep safe caches

### P1-9. Rétro-Direct (only if a direct is scheduled in the launch fortnight)

**Merges:** data-concurrency #8; api-live #3, #4; frontend #6.

**Problem (✔):**

**Gain:**

## 3. Full-page cache for anonymous HTML: design (P1-1)

### Why now

The decision recorded in `docs/TECHNIQUE.md:204-205` reasoned about latency. Under a launch spike, the constraint is capacity:
- A cache hit costs about what `/robots.txt` costs: 1.3 ms of server time against 18.5 ms for the home page.
- On the bench, rendering keeps FPM at 70–75% of CPU at 215–246 rps.
- A hit path that serves 90% of requests is estimated at **≥4× pages/s on the bench**, where Apache compression becomes the bottleneck at an estimated 900–1,300 rps, and **about 8–10× in PHP-worker (LVE) terms on o2switch**.
- It also absorbs a large share of the stampedes on misses that P0-3 does not fully remove.

**Decision: build it**, under these conditions:
- museum host only; the association site renders in 1.4–4.5 ms and is closed;
- behind the `perf.page_cache` setting, with a purge action;
- shipped after P0-3 and P1-2.

I reject letting Apache serve the cached files directly, and a CDN for HTML at launch:
- both would lose audience statistics and the time-based expiry caps;
- a CDN needs a DNS change at launch time and a second copy of the bypass rules.

### Hook

In `public/index.php`:

```php
$request  = PageCache::normalize(Request::fromGlobals()); // strips tracking params from $request->query
$response = PageCache::serve($request) ?? PageCache::store($request, Kernel::handle($request));
$response->send();
// existing stats block, unchanged (public/index.php:17-29)
```

- On a hit, `PageCache::serve` calls `I18n::set('en')` when the path starts with `/en/` (normally done at `app/Kernel.php:155-162`), so that `Stats::hit` logs the right language. The statistics block still runs on hits: the status is 200 and the type is text/html.
- `normalize()` removes `utm_*`, `fbclid`, `gclid`, `dclid`, `msclkid`, `mc_cid`, `mc_eid`, `_ga`, `_gl` and `igshid` **before rendering**. Pages that echo the query string into links can then never embed one visitor's tracking ID into a cached page.

### Bypass: never read, never store

1. The feature is off, or the method is not GET or HEAD.
2. The host is not exactly the host of `general.base_url`, or the scheme differs.
   - This guards against Host-header poisoning: `base_url()` falls back to `HTTP_HOST` (`app/helpers.php:322-334`).
   - `general.base_url` must be set in production.
   - `Vitrine\Host::matches()` and `isPreview()` always bypass.
3. **Any `sr_session` or `sr_team` cookie, or an `Authorization` header.**
   - This covers logged-in staff, CSRF tokens, flash messages, the gate password and the cart.
   - Anonymous visitors with no cookie never start a session (`app/Core/Auth.php:69`).
4. The site is closed (`Seo::closed()`, `app/Front/Seo.php:19-22`), or the maintenance flag is fresh (`app/Kernel.php:28-34`).
   - Checked on every read, so closing the site again takes effect immediately.
5. The path:
   - does not end in `/`, or has a file extension;
   - or starts (after an optional `/en`) with any of:
     - `/admin`, `/api/`, `/imprimeur`, `/apercu-association`;
     - `/boutique`, `/faire-un-don`, `/contribuer`, `/contact`, `/newsletter`, `/souvenir`;
     - `/recherche`;
     - `/interactif/retro-direct` (server time is embedded for the live sync);
     - `/video/`, `/media/`, `/pdf/`, `/partage`, `/wp-content`;
   - or is `/interactif/fil-jaune/{a}/{b}/`, where the pairs are unbounded (about 620k).
6. The query string, after normalization:
   - holds a key outside the allow-list, which is the listing parameters the public pages actually read: `page, tri, vue, f, type, decennie, cat, comp, saison, poste, objet, motif, photographe, pp`;
   - or holds more than 4 parameters;
   - or holds a value that is not `^[\p{L}\p{N} _.-]{0,40}$`.
   - So `q`, `fragment`, `apercu`, `apercu-attente`, `teaser`, the legacy `?p=` and `?s=`, and anything else render normally.

### Store only if (checked after `Kernel::handle`)

- status 200 and `Content-Type: text/html…`;
- `$res->file === null`;
- `session_status() !== PHP_SESSION_ACTIVE`, meaning no `csrf_field`, `Session::pull` or `Auth` touched the session during rendering;
- no `Set-Cookie` in `headers_list()`;
- `$res->headers` holds no `Cache-Control`, so anything the code marks `no-store` or `private` is never stored;
- the body is between 1 KB and 1 MB;
- the disk guard file `storage/cache/pages/.full` is absent.

### Key, variants, validity

- **Key:** `sha1(host | path including /en | sorted allowed query | variant)`.
- **Variants** cover the random draws found in the render path (`app/Front/Pages.php:132,144`, `app/Front/Walls.php:49-94`, `app/Services/PhotoWall.php:416,439`). The variant is chosen with `random_int` at read time:

  | Pages | Variants |
  |---|---|
  | `/` and `/en/` (slider) | 4 |
  | `/interactif/planche-contact/`, `/le-lion-illustre/`, `/mur-du-vestiaire/`, `/mosaique/` | 8 |
  | everything else | 1 |

- **Stamp**, stored in the entry's metadata rather than in the key: `Memo::stamp()` over
  - `storage/cache/index-2.php` (rewritten by every fiche save, `app/Data/Fiches.php:378`);
  - `storage/cache/derived.php`;
  - `data/categories.json`;
  - `data/media.json`;
  - `storage/settings.json`;
  - `data/i18n/en.json`;
  - the `data/collections/` directory (atomic writes rename into it, so its mtime moves on every save);
  - the `data/fiches/` directory;
  - `storage/cache/chiffres-{lang}.json`.

  That is about 10 `stat()` calls per hit.
- **Timing:**
  - `soft = created + 120 s`;
  - `hard = min(created + 600 s, next local midnight, next Rétro-Direct start or end from RetroDirect::next(), next scheduled publish_at from P0-3)`.
  - The caps cover the ticker on every page (`app/Front/Site.php:152-180`: "Ce jour-là", J-n, "En direct"), the chiffre du jour, the footer year (`templates/partials/footer.php:94`), the 100 moments and scheduled publications.
  - `countdown_html` is animated from its ISO attribute in JavaScript, so a few minutes of staleness on its initial value is harmless.
- **Read decision:**

  | Condition | Result |
  |---|---|
  | `now < soft` and the stamp is unchanged | **HIT** |
  | `now < hard`, otherwise | `flock(LOCK_EX\|LOCK_NB)` on `pages/locks/{key[0..1]}.lock` (256 lock files). The winner renders and stores (**MISS**); others get the old copy (**STALE**). |
  | no entry, or `now ≥ hard` | **MISS**: render without waiting, as today. |

  The cold burst at opening is handled by the warm-up in OPS-5.

### Storage, headers, cleanup

- **Storage:**
  - one file per entry: `storage/cache/pages/{k[0..1]}/{k}.page`, with a first line of JSON metadata (status, headers to replay, created, soft, hard, stamp, etag) followed by the body;
  - written to a temporary file and renamed;
  - never a PHP file, so HTML stays out of OPcache;
  - outside `public/`;
  - not in `KEEP_CACHES`, so a one-click update purges it automatically.
- **Headers on hits and on stored misses:**
  - the stored `Content-Type`, `Content-Security-Policy` and `X-Robots-Tag`;
  - `ETag: "<sha1(body):16>"`;
  - `Cache-Control: public, max-age=0, must-revalidate`. Browsers and the service worker revalidate, nothing is cached at the edge, and `keepable()` in `sw.js` accepts it;
  - `X-Page-Cache: HIT|STALE|MISS|BYPASS`.
- **304:** a matching `If-None-Match` gets a 304 with no body. This requires P1-2: with a per-request nonce, a 304 would carry a CSP that does not match the cached body.
- **Compression:** do not pre-compress. o2switch applies Brotli outside the PHP pool, and a PHP-set `Content-Encoding` would bypass it.
- **Cleanup:** the cron `menage` task deletes entries past `hard` that are more than 10 minutes old. Above 200 MB or 8,000 files, it writes `.full`, which stops storing until the next run. Expected working set at launch: a few hundred URLs × 1–8 variants × 15–260 KB, so 10–50 MB.
- **Kill switch:** the `perf.page_cache` setting, plus "empty `storage/cache/pages/`", added to the FTP-deploy checklist.

### Residual staleness, accepted

| Content | Maximum staleness |
|---|---|
| Onze vote results, "J'y étais" counts and similar counters | 120 s |
| Content edits | one render duration (stale is served only to concurrent requests while the winner re-renders) |
| A random slide or wall draw | 120 s, spread over 4–8 variants |
| Asset `?v=` after an FTP deploy | 120 s, unless the purge is done |

### Tests that prove it is safe (all on the scratch copy, never on `storage/settings.json` of the bench)

1. Two anonymous `GET /saisons/`: the second is a HIT, the bodies and CSP are identical, and there are no CSP errors in the console.
2. Logged-in staff (cookie): always BYPASS. The team bar and the closed-shop links still render. Nothing is written under `pages/`.
3. `/contact/`, `/contribuer/`, `/faire-un-don/`, `/boutique/`, `?q=`, `?fragment=1`, `?apercu=`: always BYPASS, and no file is created.
4. `?utm_source=x&fbclid=y` hits the same key as the clean URL, and the body contains no `fbclid`.
5. `Host: evil.example` → BYPASS.
6. Save a fiche, then make the next anonymous GET: the change is visible.
7. Set `waiting.enabled=true` on the copy: the next GET is the 503 waiting page.
8. Force `hard` to the next minute: the ticker day changes. Schedule a direct 2 minutes ahead: the ticker shows "En direct" within 1 s of the start.
9. `If-None-Match` → 304.
10. Stats: lines are added to the raw log on hits, and `/en/` hits are logged as `en`.
11. Load:
    - `node run-all.js after --only=load --scenarios=a-home,b-mix25`;
    - targets: ≥800 rps at 100 connections for a-home, p99 under 150 ms, FPM share of CPU under 30%;
    - `tests/perf.php` green.

## 4. Operational steps (no code, mandatory)

**OPS-1. OPcache on o2switch**
- The bench FPM runs 256 MB with 32 MB of interned strings; the PHP defaults here are 128/8 (`php -r ini_get`).
- Needs measured on the bench: about 92–95 MB of shared memory with no waste, and 25.4 MB of interned strings. With 8 MB, `index` plus `search` already overflow the buffer, and the memo rewrites lead to OPcache restarts (a cold start for every worker).
- Check `phpinfo()` or Système › Serveur on production.
- Ask o2switch for `memory_consumption` ≥256, `interned_strings_buffer` ≥32, `max_accelerated_files` ≥20000, and `file_cache` if allowed (cold loads measured 5–9× faster). These are system-level settings and cannot go in `.user.ini`.
- During the first launch hours, watch `wasted_memory`, `oom_restarts` and `restart_pending`.

**OPS-2. Pre-generate thumbnails on production before opening**
- `php bin/console.php images <w>` (`bin/console.php:107`) for 320, 480, 800 and 1200 at least, plus the slider pool at 800/1200/1600, and the walls through P1-10.
- Missing variants locally: about 30k derivatives, about 1 h of single-core CPU. Generating all 7 widths would take 3.5–4.3 GB of disk (check the quota and inodes).
- The command runs one width per call, so run it in batches with a time budget, or generate locally and upload `public/media/` by FTP (it is excluded from the Updater).
- Count `*.webp` files per width on production before and after.
- Update `docs/DEPLOIEMENT.md:105-109`, which currently says pre-generation is not needed.

**OPS-3. Never empty `storage/cache/` on the live site**
- Change the procedure in `docs/TECHNIQUE.md:198-199`.
- After any deploy, run `php bin/console.php index`, then `derived`, then `search`, then warm Chiffres from the CLI, before traffic arrives.
- Make sure no `storage/cache/apres-mise-a-jour` flag is pending at opening.

**OPS-4. Launch-window freeze, the first 2–4 hours**
- No back-office saves and no deploys. Until P0-3 ships, each save starts two stampede waves in every language.
- Pause the AI cron tasks (traductions, correcteur, audio).
- Keep `ai.enabled` off. The assistant holds a worker for up to two sequential Gemini calls with 45–60 s timeouts (`app/Services/Gemini.php:147`, `:180`) and has no global concurrency cap. If it must be on, add a 4-slot `LOCK_NB` semaphore and 20 s timeouts first.
- Keep the shop closed, or limit it to the 3 simple models (section 5).

**OPS-5. Opening sequence**
1. Set `general.base_url` to `https://musee.fcsochauxretro.com`.
2. Switch `waiting.enabled` off.
3. **Warm up immediately:** curl the 200 most likely URLs in fr and en (home ×8 to fill the variants, the Interactif pages, the "à la une" fiches, `/chiffres/`, `/records/`), plus `/partage/{id}.png` for the home and the top fiches.
4. Only then send the "ouverture" push, **paced**: for example 1,000 per cron tick through a smaller `process()` budget, spread over 10–20 minutes, instead of `Admin\Push` → `process(45)` in one go.

**OPS-6. Host checks**
- LVE limits: entry processes, CPU, physical memory, IO; the PHP-FPM `max_children`; `request_terminate_timeout`.
- Whether Brotli covers `text/javascript`: `curl -sI -H 'Accept-Encoding: br' …/site.js?v=…`.
- HTTP/2.
- The "photos" quality alert must show 0 missing originals. Otherwise every missing image is a PHP placeholder sent with `no-store` (`app/Services/Images.php:197-204`).

**OPS-7. Monitoring during the launch**
- Rolling p95 of `%D` in the access log, FPM busy workers (status page), OPcache status, PHP and Apache error logs, the size of `storage/ratelimit/*`, and the `X-Page-Cache` hit ratio.
- Rollback options: the page-cache kill switch, or the waiting page (503 in 2 ms).

**OPS-8. Re-measure after the fixes**
- `cd …/perf2026 && node run-all.js after && node compare.js baseline after`.
- Add these scenarios:
  - `stampede.sh`;
  - consent from many IPs;
  - Rétro-Direct presence;
  - push opens;
  - cold thumbnails;
  - an open-loop run, because autocannon's closed loop understates queueing.
- Keep the bench quiet: another agent's FPM pool contaminated two earlier runs.
